# Feet in Straps

- Most Pilates instructors finish with Feet in Straps because it’s a really nice exercise to end in
- Try not to fall into the trap of always warming down with Feet in Straps because clients think it’s a cooldown but it’s not
- Two parts:
  - Segment that works on inner thigh
  - Inner thigh strengthening and activation
- Other part of it is mobility depending on how you want to program
- If you were ending with FIS you would target the mobility
- Else, anterior pelvic stability and inner thigh activation

- Double green spring setting
- Lie down on your back, head finds the headrest, feet find the footrest
- Supine is always the exact same cues
- Reach behind you, grab your long straps and put them into your feet one leg at a time
- Aiming your feet up towards the footbar or top part of the mirror
- Allow for enough space for them to be able to find neutral spine

## Double leg lift and lower

Feet in Straps

Notes for using straps to support lower-body activation, hip mobility, and inner thigh work.

Most instructors finish with Feet in Straps because it is a strong closing exercise, but it is not always a cooldown.

Key concepts

  • Two parts: inner thigh strengthening and mobility.
  • When ending with Feet in Straps, target mobility; otherwise target anterior pelvic stability.
  • Use a double green spring setting for stable support.
  • Lie on your back with head on the headrest and feet on the footrest.
  • Grab the long straps one leg at a time and aim the feet toward the footbar or top mirror.
  • Leave enough space for a neutral spine to be maintained.

Double Leg Lift and Lower

  • When the client is at 45 degrees, the ribs should pull down.
  • Starting too high requires more hamstring length.
  • Control the descent using spinal connection, not momentum.

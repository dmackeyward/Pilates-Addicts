Short Spine

A short spine variation with detailed inversion and spinal massage notes.

  • Short Spine is a variation and an ask.
  • Not everyone is comfortable in inversion with weight over the shoulder blades.
  • The variation in the book makes inversion easier and more accessible.
  • Clients often enjoy the spinal massage effect.
  • Lengthen, tuck up toward the center, and avoid going too far over.
  • Keep the back lifted and tuck the knees toward the chest.
  • Knees land above the head and the back of the knees rest against the calves.
  • Keep everything connected and peel the spine down one vertebra at a time.
  • Use the Plea version as a variation.
  • Press up without letting the straps win.
  • Inhale to tuck the knees and exhale to lower the spine, not the knees.

# Standing Series Notes

## Inner thigh series

- Yellow spring
- Footbar doesn’t matter but a good rule of thumb is to put the footbar halfway down
- Breathing is different for each exercise

## Side splits

- Inhale to slide the carriage away
- Exhale draw the carriage back in

### Cues
- Standing up nice and tall
- Look at yourself in the mirror
- Weight on our heels
- Spine stacked nice and tall over our hips
- Weight always in one leg instead of another
- Weight distribution is equal

### Focus
- Splitting equally into both femurs
- Greater trochanter crease should appear equal
- Equal hip abduction on both sides
- Press down through your heels and pull yourself back in reaching your spine upwards towards the ceiling
- Inhale to slide the carriage away
- Think about tailbone pointing down as you spread

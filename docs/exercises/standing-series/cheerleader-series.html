Hands in Strap

  • Put this exercise into the classical group because it is not an authentic movement.
  • Teach 4 points over hands in straps for people who cannot engage abs.
  • This exercise helps people activate their abs.
  • It is harder than hands in straps, so you can't stay there for too long.
  • Instantly engages the 6-pack and lower abdominals.
  • Springs: 1 green, 1 yellow.
  • Lie in the carriage and grab your short straps.
  • Shoulder blades should be back.
  • Inhale, exhale into tabletop.
  • Starting position 1.0: dead bug, already in neutral.
  • Starting position 2.0: clasp your hands together.
  • Make a ‘plea’ position and spread your legs to the corners of the room.
  • Make a fist with your palms and reach through the middle of the diamond.
  • Use abs to connect the arms and core.
  • Inhale, exhale nod your chin to your chest.
  • Glue your heels together to activate the inner thighs and abs.
  • Use a long strap for Full Cheerleader.
  • Make sure you dial in the right springs.

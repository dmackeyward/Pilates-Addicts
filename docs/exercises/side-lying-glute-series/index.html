Side Lying Glute Series

Side-lying glute and hip work that supports alignment and stability.

Strapless Setup

  • Varieties include strap and strapless; setup is the same for both.
  • Keep the head and neck supported on a pillow without floating into the shoulder rest.
  • Place the bottom knee in close and the top heel on the footbar.
  • Align the top knee and heel with the top hip and find a stable position first.
  • Pull the bottom waist up and stack the hips.

Execution

  • Like sprinter and scooter work, once alignment is right the glutes engage strongly.
  • Inhale to prepare, exhale to slide the carriage away.
  • Inhale to bend the knee forward while keeping it in line with the thigh.
  • Exhale to press the carriage away with controlled hip extension.
  • Maintain height and avoid sagging into the reformer.
  • Stay lifted so the glutes are the primary drivers of the movement.

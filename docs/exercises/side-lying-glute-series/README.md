# Side Lying Glute Series

## Strapless

- Varieties include strap and strapless, same setup for both
- Back of neck often gets shoved up into the shoulder rest, so the setup is very important
- Use the ball as a pillow and lie on your side facing the wall, putting the side of your head on the pillow
- Just the head and neck are on the pillow
- Shuffle your bottom towards the back corner of the carriage

- From here, tuck your bottom knee in
- Place your top heel on the front padded side of the footbar
- Knee and heel inline with the top hip
- Not in neutral but positioning first
- Pulling your knee forward in front of you
- Lift the bottom waist
- Stack your hips

- Like sprinter and scooter - once you find the right alignment your glutes are firing
- Inhale to prepare, exhale to slide the carriage away
- Inhale bend the knee forward
- Inhale knee aims forward as you bend that knee keeping it in line with the thigh
- Exhale to press the carriage away
- Make sure we are not sagging into the reformer
- Not sagging so we can get the glute firing

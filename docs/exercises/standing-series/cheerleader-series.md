## Hands in strap

- Put this exercises into the classical because it’s not an authentic movement
- Teach 4 points over hands in straps for people who cannot engage abs
- This is also an exercise that you can use to help people to activate abs
- This one is harder than hands in straps so you can’t be here for that long
- Instantly goes into your 6 packs and lower abdominals when you do
- Springs: 1 green, 1 yellow
- Lie in the carriage and grab your short straps
- Shoulder blades putting back
- Inhale, exhale table top
- Starting position 1.0 - dead bug - already in your neutral
- Starting position 2.0 - clasp your hands together

- Make ‘Plea’ position, spread your legs to the corners of the room
- Make a fist with your palms
- Reaching towards
- Using abs to engage connection between arms and abs
- Inhale, exhale nod your chin to chest
- Reason you want to glue your heels together is to activate inner thigh and abs
- Reason why you clasp your hands
- Push into the straps so that your fists are facing the ceiling
- REACH your fists through the middle of the diamond - not reaching up, reaching through your legs
